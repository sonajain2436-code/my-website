Mathematical formulation, system layers, and answers to hackathon judges

1. Constrained Model Predictive Optimization

Traditional data centers run PID loops that only react after server dies overheat. Green Monitor AI solves a receding-horizon optimization problem every 60 seconds:

$$\min_{\mathbf{u}_t} \quad \sum_{k=0}^{H-1} \Big[ \alpha \cdot \text{Water}_{\text{evap}}(\mathbf{u}_{t+k}) + \beta \cdot \text{Power}_{\text{chiller}}(\mathbf{u}_{t+k}) \Big]$$
Subject to Hard Physical Constraints:
1. $\; T_{\text{die}, i}(t + k) \le T_{\text{safe}} \quad (75.0^\circ\text{C}) \quad \forall \text{ rack } i$
2. $\; \mathbf{u}_{\min} \le \mathbf{u}_t \le \mathbf{u}_{\max} \quad (\text{Flow rate: } 5 \le F \le 30 \text{ L/min})$
3. $\; T_{\text{inlet}} \ge T_{\text{dew\_point}} + 2^\circ\text{C} \quad (\text{Anti-condensation limit})$

Where u_t represents the vector of actuator setpoints (chilled water valve apertures, CRAC fan variable frequency drives, and cooling tower fan speeds). α and β allow dynamic weighting between water conservation and power cost.

3. How Water Is Saved in Cooling Towers

In typical evaporative cooling towers, for every kilowatt-hour of heat dissipated into the atmosphere, approximately 1.5 to 2.5 liters of water evaporate into thin air.

Evaporation Reduction

By preventing unneeded over-cooling in idle/moderate racks, total heat dumped to the cooling tower is minimized, directly reducing required latent heat evaporation (m_evap = Q / h_fg).

Economizer Free-Air Utilization

When wet-bulb temperature is favorable (< 18°C), the system routes air directly through heat exchangers, bypassing evaporative cooling towers entirely.

4. Defending Tough Questions from Hackathon Judges

Q1: What if the AI model makes a wrong prediction and a GPU overheats?

A: Green Monitor AI operates as an advisory supervisory layer. The physical Building Management System (BMS) retains a hardwired safety watchdog. If any rack approaches within 3°C of the 75°C threshold, the hardware governor immediately overrides the AI setpoint and forces 100% full emergency cooling. Server integrity is never compromised.

Q2: Are these water savings real or theoretical?

A: In this prototype, savings are computed using empirical thermodynamic formulas validated by ASHRAE TC 9.9 standards for liquid and air cooling. In a production pilot, savings are measured against calibrated magnetic flow meters on the cooling tower makeup line.

Q3: Why not just run fans at a fixed medium speed?

A: Modern AI training jobs create massive, sudden thermal spikes (e.g. 70B parameter LLM backpropagation kicks all 8 GPUs from 200W to 700W each in seconds). Static cooling either under-cools during peak load (causing thermal throttling) or constantly over-cools during idle periods (wasting thousands of liters of water).
